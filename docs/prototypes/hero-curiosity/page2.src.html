Ukiyoe: Layer by Layer (existing card)

A dry garden of interests

枯山水の見立てで作り直した。石・砂紋・苔・飛石・落ち葉の一つひとつが、関心を1つずつ表す。主役は置かず、重心を中心より右に寄せ、左と右上は空けておく。色は墨と砂の灰色に、落ち葉の朽葉色を1つだけ。
Rebuilt as a dry landscape garden. Each stone, ripple, patch of moss, stepping stone and fallen leaf stands for one interest. There is no single hero: the weight sits right of centre, and the left and upper right stay empty. Ink and sand greys, with one dry-leaf ochre.

動きは庭を作る順番に沿う。砂を左から右へ掃き、石を置き、波紋が広がり、苔と飛石が現れ、最後に葉が一枚落ちて止まる。止まった後は、何も動かない。
The motion follows the order a garden is made in: the sand is raked from left to right, the stones are set, ripples spread, moss and stepping stones appear, and finally a single leaf falls and comes to rest. After that, nothing moves.
